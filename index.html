Basics
powers x^2 · implicit multiply 2x, 3sin(x)
+ - * / ^ · parens · |x| absolute value
factorial 5! · x!
constants pi π e tau

Trig
sin cos tan sec csc cot
inverse asin acos atan asec acsc acot
asin/acos exist only on −1…1
atan spans −π/2…π/2 · acos returns 0…π
plotting outside a domain just leaves a gap
poles leave a gap too — tan(x) and x^(-1/3)
break rather than joining with a vertical line

Hyperbolic
sinh cosh tanh sech csch coth
inverse asinh acosh atanh
acosh needs x≥1, atanh needs −1<x<1

Exponentials & logs
exp exp2 ln log log2 logb(b,x)
ln = natural, log = base 10

Roots & powers
sqrt cbrt nthroot(n,x) pow(a,b)
hypot(a,b)
negative bases work for fractional powers when the
exponent has an odd denominator: x^(2/3) plots for x < 0,
x^(1/2) does not (no real root)

Rounding & step-like
floor ceil round trunc frac sign
mod(a,b) min(a,b) max(a,b) clamp(x,lo,hi) lerp(a,b,t)
frac(x) = x − floor(x) — try frac(x) for a sawtooth

Special

Calculus
deriv(f) deriv2(f) deriv3(f)
Differentiated symbolically, so it's exact. Try deriv(x^3) → 3x², or deriv(sin(x)) → cos(x)
